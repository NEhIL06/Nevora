NEVORA — Brand Document
1. Brand Name
NEVORA
2. Tagline
New Era of Everyday Food
3. Brand Category
Modern Indian Food & Millet Brand
Nevora brings traditional Indian grains, millets, snacks, and everyday foods into a modern, convenient, and premium format.

4. Brand Essence
Tradition, made relevant for today.
Nevora is built around the idea that healthy, traditional Indian food doesn't have to feel old-fashioned, complicated, or boring.
We take familiar ingredients and foods—millets, atta, rice, snacks, cookies, noodles, pasta and more—and present them in a way that fits modern everyday life.

5. Vision
To make better everyday food a natural part of every Indian household.
Nevora aims to build a modern Indian food brand rooted in traditional grains while creating products that are accessible, convenient and enjoyable.

6. Mission
Make traditional Indian nutrition easier to choose, easier to consume, and more relevant to modern lifestyles.
We focus on:
* Quality ingredients
* Familiar Indian foods
* Millet-based innovation
* Convenient formats
* Modern packaging
* Honest communication
* Everyday affordability

7. Brand Positioning
Nevora is not just a millet brand.
It is a modern everyday food brand built around India's traditional food ecosystem.
From raw grains to convenient snacks and everyday staples, Nevora connects traditional Indian food with modern consumption.

9. Target Customer
Primary
Urban and semi-urban Indian households looking for better everyday food choices without sacrificing taste or convenience.
Secondary
* Young professionals
* Health-conscious families
* Parents
* Fitness/wellness consumers
* People exploring millets
* Consumers looking for alternatives to conventional snacks
Age
18–45, with household purchasing influence extending beyond this range.

10. Brand Personality
Nevora should feel:
Modern
Clean, contemporary and relevant.
Natural
Rooted in grains, ingredients and Indian food culture.
Trustworthy
Simple claims. Transparent communication.
Premium but approachable
High-quality appearance without feeling unaffordable or elitist.
Optimistic
Food should feel exciting, not restrictive.

11. Brand Voice
We sound:
* Simple
* Confident
* Warm
* Modern
* Conversational
* Informative
We avoid:
* Overly technical health claims
* Fear-based marketing
* Excessive "diet food" messaging
* Fake wellness promises
* Complicated language
* Overly traditional/dated presentation
Example:
Instead of:
"A scientifically formulated superfood snack for optimal nutritional performance."
Say:
"The crunch you love, made with the goodness of ragi."

12. Visual Direction
Overall Look
Premium Natural + Modern Indian
Think:
Indian roots × contemporary design × clean food photography
Packaging
The established Nevora packaging direction should maintain:
* Clear/transparent pouch where appropriate
* Product visibly identifiable
* Front label positioned toward the lower/front portion
* Generous transparent area
* Clean typography
* Minimal visual clutter
* Premium-natural appearance
* Strong Nevora branding
This keeps the actual food visible, which supports authenticity and product appeal.

13. Core Brand Message
One-line
Nevora brings India's traditional foods into the modern everyday kitchen.
Short version
Traditional ingredients. Modern food. Everyday life.
Consumer-facing idea
Eat familiar. Choose better. Live modern.

15. Brand Story
Nevora started with a simple thought:
India already has incredible food.
Millets, grains, traditional recipes and ingredients have been part of our food culture for generations.
But the way we consume food is changing.
We wanted to build a brand that brings these traditional foods into today's lifestyle—with better products, modern packaging, convenient formats and a brand people actually want to bring home.
That's Nevora.
A new era of everyday food.

17. Brand North Star
Make traditional Indian food feel modern, desirable and everyday.
That should be the filter for every product, package, reel, poster, store display and advertisement.
One important practical note: a quick web search shows that “Nevora” is already used by unrelated businesses/brands, including wellness, cosmetics, automotive and other companies. So before investing heavily in the identity, packaging inventory, signage, and digital assets, it would be sensible to do a proper India trademark search specifically for your food/Nice classes.
